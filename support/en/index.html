Support

How to use TradeRecall, and what to do if something goes wrong.

Contact us

Email: support.traderecall@gmail.com

You can also write to us from the app: Settings > Contact. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened (steps to reproduce, if possible). Replies may take a few days.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. It is never sent to a server. This means your data is erased if you switch phones or delete the app, so please make backups.

How do I make a backup?

Go to Settings > Data > Backup and tap "Export", then choose where to save the file (Files, cloud storage, etc.). On a new device, open the same screen and tap "Import" to select that file. Importing replaces your current data with the contents of the backup.

How do I find trades by tag?

In "Entry reason", "Exit reason" or "Notes", write a line that starts with "#" (for example, #trend). It becomes a tag you can find later.

How do I hide my amounts?

Tap the eye icon at the top right of the calendar to hide amounts as "****".

How do I add or remove products (such as USD/JPY)?

Go to Settings > Products. Deleting a product does not delete past trades; they appear as "Not set" (gray) in the graph.

What are the Rules tab and trader levels?

In the Rules tab you can write your own trading rules. On each trade, you choose how well you followed them (five choices from 0-20% to 80-100%). Your trader level (Lv.1 to Lv.5) is based on the average of your last 100 rated trades; at least 10 rated trades are needed. The Graph tab (All together) shows your profit/loss for each level.

Why does the app show ads?

TradeRecall is free and shows ads on some screens (Calendar, Graph and Settings). The first time ads are shown, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Images are large / I want to keep full quality

When "Save images in a smaller size" is ON, new images are compressed to about 200 KB. Turn it OFF if you prefer quality. Changing it does not affect images you have already saved.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings > Data > Delete all data. This cannot be undone, so make a backup first.

Can I trade in the app?

No. TradeRecall is a record-keeping and review tool. It does not place trades or give investment advice.
