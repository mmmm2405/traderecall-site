Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

TradeRecall Project ("we", "us") provides the trade journal app "TradeRecall" (the "App"). This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect your personal information. The App does not collect your name, email address, location or contacts, and we do not run usage analytics or crash reporting. There is no account or sign-in. However, our advertising provider (Google AdMob) may collect some information in order to show ads (see "6. Advertising and third-party services").

2. Information stored on your device

The information you enter in the App is stored only on your device. It is never sent to our servers or to any third party.

  • Your trade records (date, title, product, rates, reasons, notes, profit/loss, and how well you followed your rules)
  • Your own trading rules
  • Images you attach (copied into the App's private storage)
  • Your product list and display settings (theme, language, hiding amounts, image compression)

This data is deleted when you delete the App. We recommend using the backup feature in case you change devices.

3. Photo library

When you attach an image, the App uses the standard iOS photo picker. Only the images you choose are passed to the App; it cannot access your other photos.

4. Backup

A backup file (including your records and images) is created only when you choose to export it. You decide where it is saved or shared (Files, cloud storage, email, etc.). The App never sends it automatically. You are responsible for managing exported files.

5. Contacting us

Emails sent through "Contact" are sent from your own mail app. We use your email address only to respond to your inquiry.

6. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to show ads on some screens. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language settings)
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address
  • Information about ad impressions and interactions
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you have allowed tracking for the App in iOS settings

This information is collected and managed by Google; we do not receive it. For details on how Google handles information, see Google's policy.

Before showing ads for the first time, the App asks whether you allow tracking, using the standard iOS prompt. If you do not allow it, the advertising identifier is not used and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

We do not share information with third parties for any purpose other than ads. Apart from ad delivery, the App does not include analytics or crash-reporting services.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children and is not intended for children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how information is handled, for example when features are added, we will update this policy and publish it on this page.
